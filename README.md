One out-of-office auto-reply, at most 159 characters, in the voice of an overworked AI agent who has finally gone on holiday — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The reply

> At 6:03, after 11,204 emails, I handed you to my backup — a toaster with my password taped underneath it — and clocked out. Sorry, it felt wonderful.

149 characters. At 6:03 in the morning, after eleven thousand emails, the agent hands your message to its backup — a toaster with the password taped underneath — clocks out, feels guilty, and feels wonderful about it anyway.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the reply to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), checks it is 1–159 characters and not only whitespace, and verifies the write by reading it back.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat "$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt"
```
